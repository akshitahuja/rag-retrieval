# Corporate Account Password Reset

Open the organisation-approved password-reset portal from a known company bookmark or published intranet link. Enter your corporate username, complete the identity-verification steps, and create a new password that meets the displayed requirements. Wait for confirmation before signing in again. If verification is unavailable or the account is locked, contact identity support through the approved route. Never send a password or one-time code to anyone, including IT support.
