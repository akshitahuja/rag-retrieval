# Printer Connectivity and Printing Troubleshooting

Check that the printer is powered on, ready, and has paper and toner. Confirm that the computer is on the approved corporate network and that the intended printer is selected. Review the print queue for paused or stuck jobs and cancel only your own duplicate jobs. Restart the application and submit a one-page test print. If still unavailable, restart your computer. In a ticket, provide the printer name or asset label, your device name, location, error, and whether other users are affected. Do not install drivers from unofficial sources.
