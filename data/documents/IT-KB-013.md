# Recognising and Reporting Suspicious Emails

Be cautious of unexpected attachments, urgent password or payment requests, unfamiliar sender addresses, mismatched links, and requests to bypass normal approvals. Do not open unexpected attachments or enter credentials after following an email link. Use the approved Report Phishing feature or security-reporting process. If you entered your password or approved an unexpected MFA prompt, contact the security team immediately through a known channel. Do not forward suspicious content informally or delete it before following the reporting process.
