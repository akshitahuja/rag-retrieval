# How to Raise an IT Support Ticket

Open the approved IT service portal. Choose Report an Issue for something broken or Request a Service for new access or equipment. Enter a specific summary and describe what you were doing, what happened, and when it started. Include the affected application, device name, business impact, and exact error text. Attach screenshots only when permitted and ensure they contain no passwords or sensitive data. Submit the ticket and save its reference number. Add updates to the same ticket and follow the published escalation process for urgent incidents.
