# Remote Desktop and VDI Connection Steps

Connect to the corporate network or VPN if required. Open the approved virtual desktop client or portal, sign in with your corporate account, complete MFA, and select your assigned desktop. Wait for the session to load before opening applications. If the session fails, check internet and VPN connectivity, sign out of a stale session if that option is available, and retry. Record any session or authentication error. Include your device name, virtual desktop name, and time in a ticket. Never share session links or access another user's desktop.
