# Corporate Wi-Fi Connection Troubleshooting

Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled and Airplane Mode is off. Check whether other approved devices can connect in the same area. Move closer to the access point, toggle Wi-Fi off and on, and reconnect to the ExampleCorp-Staff network. If prompted, sign in using your corporate account. If connected but unable to reach websites, check whether VPN is required for the resource. When reporting the issue, include your location, device name, time, and exact error. Do not change managed network settings or access network equipment.
