# Troubleshooting Teams Audio and Camera

Check that the microphone and camera are connected and not physically muted or covered. In the meeting application's device settings, select the correct microphone, speaker, and camera. Check operating-system privacy settings to ensure the application has permission. Close other applications that may be using the devices, then restart the meeting application and rejoin. Test the devices using an approved utility. If the issue continues, report the device model, application version, and whether audio, video, or both are affected. Do not install third-party driver tools.
