# Safe Restart and Update of a Company Laptop

Save open documents and close applications. Ensure important work is stored in an approved company location and connect the laptop to power if its battery is low. Use the operating system's normal Restart command. After restart, check the organisation-approved update settings and install updates according to company policy. If the device is stuck during an update, displays a recovery message, or repeatedly fails to boot, avoid repeated forced shutdowns and contact IT support with the device name, screen message, and time. Do not disable endpoint management or security controls.
