# Corporate VPN Setup and Connection Troubleshooting

Confirm internet access and ensure the approved VPN client is installed. Open the client, choose the ExampleCorp Remote Access profile, sign in with your corporate account, and complete MFA. Wait for the Connected status before opening internal resources. If connection fails, check internet access, restart the VPN client, and retry once. If MFA does not arrive, check the authenticator app. Record the error, device name, and time in an IT ticket. Never share your password or MFA code.
