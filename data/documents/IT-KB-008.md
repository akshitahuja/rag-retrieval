# Setting Up Multi-Factor Authentication

Open the approved identity or account-security portal and sign in. Choose the option to add an authentication method, then follow the instructions to register the approved authenticator app or security key. Complete the test verification and review available recovery methods according to company policy. If a QR code expires, restart registration from the approved portal. If your device is lost or replaced, contact identity support. Never approve a sign-in you did not initiate or share MFA codes, recovery codes, or setup QR images.
